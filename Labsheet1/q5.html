Classic Masala Chai Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 cup water
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tsp black tea leaves
  • 1 tsp crushed ginger
  • 2 green cardamoms (crushed)
  • Sugar to taste

Instructions

  1. Boil water in a saucepan along with crushed ginger and cardamom.
  2. Add tea leaves and simmer for 2-3 minutes.
  3. Pour in milk and sugar, then bring to a boil.
  4. Strain through a sieve into cups and serve hot.
